Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. About this project

Creators G is a personal content project operated by an individual creator. It is not currently a company or a service offered to third parties.

2. Information accessed through platform APIs

If the project uses a platform API such as the Pinterest API, it may access information that the account owner authorizes, such as account information, boards, Pins, publishing-related data, and available performance or analytics information. Access is limited to what is necessary for the project's authorized functionality.

3. How information is used

Information accessed through APIs is used to manage the owner's own content, publish or organize content where permitted, and understand content performance. It is not sold or rented to third parties.

4. Data sharing

The project does not intentionally sell personal information or provide authorized platform data to data brokers. Information may be processed by the relevant platform or hosting provider as necessary to provide their services.

5. Data security and retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect information under the owner's control. API credentials and access tokens should be stored securely and should not be published in public repositories. Data is retained only for as long as needed for the project's legitimate purposes or as required by applicable law.

6. Your choices

The account owner can revoke an application's access through the relevant platform's account settings. Questions about this policy can be sent through the GitHub profile.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the project changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
